# 💫 About Me:
As a B.Tech ECE student, I sit at the intersection of hardware and software. I am passionate about bridging the gap between physical systems and digital intelligence, which drives my work as a Robot & Bot Builder and Web & AI Developer. I am also interested in Data Science and Cybersecurity. Beyond the code, I am deeply involved in the tech community as a Marketing Core Member for Google Developer Groups (GDG) BPPIMT, where I help bridge the gap between technology and people. Apart from the technical side, I am a passionate storyteller. I am an amateur Video Editor, Graphic Designer, Music Producer and Filmmaker, always exploring how creativity and technology can work together. Currently, I am focused on deepening my engineering fundamentals as a GATE aspirant while continuing to build innovative projects. I'm always open to connecting with fellow engineers, developers and tech enthusiasts.
